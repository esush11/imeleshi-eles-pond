Build a single standalone HTML file called ascii-christmas.html that I open in Chrome on my laptop: a detailed 3D ASCII Christmas tree that spins, drawn on one canvas entirely with monospace characters (DM Mono), in the style of the classic spinning ASCII donut (donut.c).

3D TREE (the main thing)
- Build the tree from 4 stacked, overlapping cone tiers (tallest and widest at the bottom). Each tier has 4 flared branch layers (the radius flares out at the bottom of each layer) and wavy, ruffled edges (radius wobbles with sin(9θ) and sin(23θ)) so it looks like real branches.
- Sample tens of thousands of points over the tree surface with normals, plus a trunk cylinder, 5 present boxes around the base (with gold ribbon crosses) and a faint snowy ground disc.
- Every frame: rotate everything around the vertical axis (slow auto-spin; I can drag to spin it with inertia, the front of the tree follows my finger), tilt the camera down ~15°, perspective-project into a character grid (cells about 1/78 of the screen height), keep the closest point per cell (z-buffer), light it with a directional light from the upper front-left, and pick each character from the brightness ramp " .,-~:;=!*#$@". Darken the underside of each branch layer so the layers read as ridges. Colour by brightness: deep green to mint for the tree, brown trunk, coloured presents, pale blue snow.
- Ornaments (~50 o @ O 0 in red, gold, orange, pink and blue) and two spiral garlands of fairy lights wrapping the tree are 3D points on the surface: only draw them when they're in front (z-buffer test), with a glow, bigger when closer. The lights chase upwards.
- Star on top: a big 5-point star rasterised from characters (# in the centre, then *, then +) using a proper point-in-polygon test, facing the camera and spinning on its own axis (squash its width with cos), with a soft glow and slowly rotating rays.

SCENE
Night sky (navy gradient, twinkling . + * stars, the odd shooting star), a big shaded ASCII moon top-right, falling snow, and "merry christmas" in a gold italic serif (Cormorant Garamond) with a small mono line underneath.

ANIMATION (~15 seconds, R replays)
1. Ground and trunk appear, then the tree grows from the bottom up while spinning, with a scrambling front edge and a soft typewriter tick. While it grows, play shimmery original music: a soft swelling pad (C maj9 → F maj9 → G9, detuned sine + triangle through a lowpass that slowly opens) with twinkling high pentatonic notes through a feedback echo that get faster as the tree grows, and a rising sparkle run as the star arrives, then crossfade into the music box.
2. The star drops on with a little bounce and a shimmer of bells.
3. Ornaments pop on from the bottom up with little bell notes; the garlands start chasing; presents pop in; snow starts; the message fades in.
4. Santa's sleigh (ASCII reindeer pulling a red sleigh) flies from in front of the moon across the top of the sky, above the tree, trailing gold sparkles, with sleigh bells. It comes back every ~25 seconds, or with J / the sleigh button.
5. A music box plays Jingle Bells (public domain).

HAND GESTURE MODE
A "light it with my hands" button on the start screen (and a "hand play" pill, and ?hand in the URL) turns on the webcam and MediaPipe Hand Landmarker (tasks-vision 0.10.14 from jsDelivr, GPU with CPU fallback, 1 hand, checked about 20 times a second):
- Swipe: the palm's sideways speed spins the tree in the same direction, then it eases back to the slow auto-spin.
- Pinch thumb and index: a burst of coloured sparkles at my fingertip with a bell.
- Open palm held for ~1 second: the sleigh flies (8 second cooldown).
- Fist: a snowstorm while I hold it.
- A glowing star follows my index fingertip, a small mirrored webcam preview with gold hand dots sits top-right, and a status pill says what it sees ("show me your hand", "spinning →", "sparkles", "hold for the sleigh…", "snowstorm").

INTERACTION + CONTROLS
Start screen with a small ASCII tree, "light the tree" and "light it with my hands" (clicking turns the sound on). Click for a burst of sparkles. Space = heavier snow, O = ornament colours (classic, icy, pink), S = save PNG, F = full screen, H = hide. Frosted pills along the bottom: replay, sound, sleigh, snow, ornaments, message (edit the two lines of text), camera icon, record clip (.mp4 if supported, otherwise .webm, with the sound), full screen, hand play, hide. Keep it fast: precompute the points once, draw characters grouped by colour, glows from pre-rendered radial-gradient sprites, cap device pixel ratio at 2, no CSS blur filters. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Nothing is uploaded.

BUGS I RAN INTO (handle these from the start):
- the tree looked flat, like a 2D drawing → render it as a real 3D object the donut.c way: points on the surface, rotate, project, z-buffer, and pick each character by brightness
- the whole tree was a solid block of # and @ → darken the underside of each branch layer and make the light less even, so it shows ridges and a shadow side
- the star came out as a blob → build it from a proper 5-point star shape with a point-in-polygon test and tone down its glow
- the sleigh flew off the top of the screen, then through the tree → leave room at the top and fly it from in front of the moon, above the tree top
- the snowy ground stretched into flat lines → make the ground disc smaller and tilt the camera down about 15°
- dragging spun the tree the opposite way → flip the sign so the front of the tree follows your finger (and your hand)

have fun making yours :) save the file, open it in Chrome, and tag me if you post it.
