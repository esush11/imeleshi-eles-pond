Build a single standalone HTML file called bloom-by-smile.html that I open in Chrome on my laptop. It's a storybook garden that grows along the bottom of my mirrored webcam: tender, playful, a little magic. Use MediaPipe Face Landmarker (tasks-vision 0.10.14 from jsDelivr, GPU with CPU fallback) with face blendshapes.

Smile: when I smile, flowers sprout from the bottom edge on curving stems with leaves and the odd curly tendril, grow, form a bud and pop open with little white sparkles. Use six hand-drawn flower kinds with soft gradients and a gentle glow: cosmos, daisy, layered peony, tulip, bluebell and forget-me-not clusters, in blush, peony, buttercup, lilac and forget-me-not colours. Stems sway, a strip of grass sways along the bottom, and the bottom of the screen glows pink. Once 6+ flowers are in bloom, butterflies flutter in and visit them, and glowing motes drift up from the blooms. Cap it at 60 flowers (revive the most wilted one instead of adding more).

Frown: when I frown, stems droop, colours fade to dusty tones, petals drop and drift to the ground, the screen dims slightly grey and the butterflies fly away. Detect frowns against a baseline: on start, learn my neutral face over ~50 frames, then compare mouth-corner drop, mouthFrown, browDown, browInnerUp, mouthShrugLower and mouthPress against it. Ignore frowns while I'm smiling. Smiling again brings the garden back.

UI: a frosted glass card top-left with the title in Great Vibes, live smile and frown bars with a threshold line, a status line ("smile to grow", "blooming…", "wilting…", "relax your face… learning") and "X flowers in bloom". Controls bottom-right as glass pills: smile and frown sensitivity sliders, a camera picker dropdown, Recalibrate, Backdrop (swaps the camera for a plain blush-pink background), Reset, Fullscreen, Hide UI (H). Hold S to fake a smile and D to fake a frown. Intro screen: "bloom by smile" / "Smile and the garden grows. Frown and the flowers wilt." / "Open the garden", plus a note to keep a relaxed face for two seconds. Fonts: Great Vibes + DM Sans. Ask for the camera at up to 4K. Nothing is uploaded.

Also make bloom-by-smile-phone.html: front camera by default with a Flip camera button, fits a vertical screen and the iPhone notch (safe areas), full-width card and centred controls, Hide gives a clean screen and tapping anywhere brings the controls back, max 38 flowers on narrow screens, and a clear message if the camera is blocked or the page isn't on https. Tell me how to put it online with Netlify Drop so my iPhone can open it.

BUGS I RAN INTO (handle these from the start):
- my frowns didn't register → learn my neutral face for ~2 seconds first and compare frowns against it, with smile and frown sensitivity sliders
- smiling sometimes counted as frowning → ignore frowns while a smile is detected
- it got laggy once the garden filled up → cap it at 60 flowers (38 on phones) and revive the most wilted one instead of adding more
- the camera was blocked on my phone → the page has to be on https (e.g. Netlify), not opened as a file

have fun making yours :) save the file, open it in Chrome, and tag me if you post it.
