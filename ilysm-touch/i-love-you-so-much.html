I love you so much

Touch a fingertip to your thumb to say its word.
Left hand: index = I, middle = love, ring = you.
Right hand: index = so, middle = much.

Word sounds

Every word uses your browser's voice until you record your own. Tap ● and say the word. It records for 1.5 seconds, or tap ■ to stop sooner. You can also upload a clip with ⤒.
Off = only the word sounds go into your clip, with the cleanest, fastest audio. Turning it on keeps the mic open, which can make playback laggy and crunchy, especially on a phone or with Bluetooth headphones. Only use it if a word still uses the browser voice.

Pinch detection

Move left if words fire when you don't mean them to. Move right if your pinches are being missed.
