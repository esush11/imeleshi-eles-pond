Build a single standalone HTML file called rainy-window.html that I open in Chrome or Arc on my laptop: a cosy fogged-up window on a rainy afternoon, with my mirrored webcam behind the glass, full-bleed.

Glass and fog: show the camera with a slight blue-grey tint. Over it, a frost layer (the camera blurred and brightened, with a pale white wash) masked by a fog canvas, so wherever there's fog the glass looks steamed up. Start fully fogged. A "refog" pill cycles off / slow / fast (off by default); C fogs it all again; holding Space adds fog.

Breath: listen on the mic (no echo cancellation or noise suppression), track the room's noise floor, and when I blow, bloom soft fog patches just below my mouth, spreading sideways like real breath. Find my mouth with MediaPipe Face Detection, sending a frame every 5th turn alongside the hand tracker; if it fails, bloom from the centre. Show a small breath meter with a sensitivity slider, and a mic picker.

Hands (MediaPipe Hands, one hand, loaded first, toast "Hand tracking ready. Pinch to write."): pinch thumb and index to write. Measure the pinch in 3D relative to palm size, with a "pinch" looseness slider and a dot that lights up. Each stroke wipes a crisp, almost fully clear line (smoothed curves), with a little glowing star following my fingers. Make a fist to wipe a big patch (1.6x hand width) like a sleeve, shown as a dotted circle. Only count a fist when all four fingertips are closer to the wrist than their knuckles for 2 frames, so a pinch is never read as a fist. I can also write by dragging with the mouse or a finger. Brush size slider.

Raindrops: realistic lenses. Each drop shows a shrunken, upside-down, slightly brightened view of the camera, with a dark rim, a bright caustic at the bottom and a small glint on top. Fine mist, many small static drops, and bigger drops that run down in stop-start wobbly paths, clear trails in the fog, leave tiny droplets behind and swallow the static drops they hit. Soft generated rain sound with a "rain: on/off" pill.

UI: frosted pills (brush, breath, pinch bottom-left; mic, reset writing, tracking dots, refog, rain, full screen, hide, and a camera shutter that saves a PNG bottom-right). "Tracking dots" shows the pinch points with their value and a "mouth" circle. Hint pill at the top that fades after 9 seconds. Start screen: "rainy window" in Caveat over a rainy gradient, "open the window" button that goes full screen straight from the click. F toggles full screen, H hides controls (two-finger tap brings them back), S saves a picture, Escape stops. If a phone camera like Camo or Continuity Camera is connected, use it. Fonts: Caveat + DM Sans. Nothing leaves my computer.

BUGS I RAN INTO (handle these from the start):
- blowing didn't fog anything because the mic cleaned my breath out → turn off echo cancellation and noise suppression on the mic and track the room's noise floor
- a pinch sometimes counted as a fist and wiped a huge patch → only count a fist when all four fingertips are closer to the wrist than their knuckles for 2 frames in a row
- the writing was backwards → mirror the webcam so it moves like a real mirror
- full screen wouldn't open → ask for full screen straight from the button click, not after the camera loads
- camera and mic were blocked in Claude's preview → download the HTML file and open it in Chrome or Arc

have fun making yours :) save the file, open it in Chrome, and tag me if you post it.
